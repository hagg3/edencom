About and history

Eden: World Builder

Eden: World Builder is a voxel sandbox for iPhone and iPad made by Ari Ronen (Kingly Games) and released on the App Store in 2010. It was the first well-known block-building game on iOS, about a year before Minecraft: Pocket Edition. It stayed high in the charts for years.

It had a few things of its own: bouncy physics, ice slides, controls designed for touch, and Get Worlds, a list inside the game where you could browse other players' worlds and upload your own.

The source, and this fork

The source for version 2.1 (2015) was made public in 2018. This project, Eden: Community Edition, grew from that code. The official game has had closed-source updates since: a new texture pack and a taller world in 2021, then multiplayer, new blocks, paintable signs, command blocks and an adventure mode in 2026.

The fork keeps the classic sandbox and its art style. It fixes bugs, brings multiplayer and the newer blocks across, adds a mod and console system, and runs on Mac, Windows and Linux as well as iPad and iPhone. The engine is now plain C++ with its own small renderer; the original was iterated on, not remade.

Not the official game

This is a community project. It is not made by, or endorsed by, Ari Ronen or Kingly Games. The official Eden Discord is owned by Ari and run by community members, and is the best place to ask questions: join the Discord.
