Eden World Builder, still building

A community fork of Eden: World Builder by Ari Ronen, from the 2018 source release. Not affiliated with the original. Say hi on the official Discord.

Build anything, block by block, in the world you remember. Eden: Community Edition keeps Eden: World Builder's worlds, blocks and feel, and brings it to Mac, Windows, Linux and iPad, with multiplayer, mods and a few new tricks.

Download

Remember Eden?

If you built on an iPod touch or an iPad around 2011, this is that game. Your old .eden worlds import here, the blocks and paint colours are the same, and the default map is the one you started on.

  • Same: the blocks, the colours, TNT, portals, creatures, sharing worlds.
  • New: desktop with keyboard and mouse, multiplayer, a hotbar, weather, mod blocks.

What you can do

Build

Place, paint and burn blocks in a 2880×2880 world, or start from scratch.

Explore

Day and night, rain and snow, caves, lava and creatures.

Share

Download worlds from Get Worlds, or build together on a server.
