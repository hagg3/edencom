Help and tutorials

Everything you need to start playing, and to find the less obvious things. Want to make your own blocks or scripts? That is on the Modding page. Stuck? The Discord is the place to ask.

Your first world

  1. Start the game. The home screen has a world list and the tiles Settings, Mods, Multiplayer and Get Worlds.
  2. Pick a world and press Play. The first world is the original Eden map, 2880 blocks square.
  3. Or make your own: choose a new world and set a name and, if you like, a seed (a number or any words; blank keeps the one shown). You can also set Height, Scale, Hilliness, Caves, Trees, Flowers, Clouds and Size, and Random rolls a new seed. Play starts it.
  4. Walk, look around, and start digging. Save game in the pause menu saves your world.

The pause menu (Esc, or Start on a gamepad) has: Resume, Save game, Warp home, Take photo, Settings, Console and Quit to menu.

Gamepad

Plug in a controller and it works, unless you switch Gamepad off in Settings. Left stick moves (click it to run faster) and the right stick looks. The right trigger mines and the left trigger builds. A jumps, B crouches, clicking the right stick flies down. X opens the block picker, D-pad up is the fire tool and D-pad down the colour picker. The shoulder buttons and D-pad left and right step through the hotbar. Start or Back opens the menu. Gamepad deadzone helps if the camera drifts.

Touch (iPhone and iPad)

These are the original Eden controls: a thumb stick on the screen moves you, dragging anywhere else turns the camera, and a short tap in the world acts in the current mode (mine, build, paint, burn or pick). Buttons on screen change the mode and open the pickers. Settings has the look speed, smoothing and glide to taste.

If the game guesses wrong about whether you have a touch screen or a mouse, set Input mode in Settings to Touch or Keyboard+Mouse. The menus work in every mode.

Building and painting

  • Mine and build. Aim at a block and click (or tap). Mining removes it; building puts the selected block against the face you are aiming at. You cannot build a block inside yourself.
  • Choose a block. Open the block picker (E) and pick one. Blocks come in shapes such as ramps and half blocks as well as cubes, and there are special blocks (doors, signs, command blocks, lamps, water, lava and more).
  • Paint. Open the colour picker (C) and choose one of the 54 colours. In paint mode, clicking a block colours it. A block you build while a colour is chosen is placed already painted.
  • Copy what you see. Middle click on a block puts that block, with its colour, in your hand and on the hotbar.
  • Burn. The fire tool sets things alight: wood, vines and some creatures burn. Two Experiments switches let fire also melt ice and char vines.

The hotbar

Nine quick slots for blocks and colours, saved with each world. It is on by default with a keyboard and mouse and off on touch screens: turn it on or off in Settings, Gameplay, Hotbar.

  • Choose a slot with 1 to 9, the mouse wheel, or the gamepad shoulder buttons.
  • Anything you pick lands on the bar by itself: in a slot that already holds it, else in the first empty slot.
  • To fill a slot on purpose, select it again (or click an empty one) to open the picker for that slot, then choose. With a picker open and the mouse over a block, press a number to put that block in that slot.
  • To empty a slot, right click it while the cursor is free, or press and hold it on touch.

Flying

Flying is off until you allow it: Settings, Gameplay, Allow fly. After that V cycles Off, Fly and Noclip (noclip passes through blocks). Space goes up and Ctrl goes down. The fly sliders (acceleration, top speed, braking, rise speed, noclip speed) appear beside the switch.

Get Worlds and sharing

Get Worlds on the home screen lists worlds made by players. It reads from three places, which you choose with the tabs: Archive (the community archive), Current server and Legacy server. Featured and Recent change the list, and Search looks for a name. Select a world to see its preview, then Download. Some worlds are very large (over a gigabyte), so watch your disk; Cancel stops a download.

The Share screen on a world uploads it to the online list or exports it to an .eden file. To bring in a world you already have as a file, see Download.

Multiplayer

  1. On the home screen choose Multiplayer.
  2. + Add a server by its address (and a label if you like). The list shows whether each server is online, how many players it has, and whether it needs a password. Refresh checks them again.
  3. Select one and press Join. The first time, the game asks for your player name. A server with a password asks for it, and can remember it for you.
  4. Press T to chat, or / to start a chat command. The console's Chat and Players tabs show the same.

In a multiplayer session you build in the server's shared world, and mod blocks are not available.

Settings

Open them from the home screen or the pause menu. The groups:

GroupWhat is in it
GameplayHealth, auto-jump, creatures, hotbar, Allow fly and the fly sliders. World settings: day/night cycle and day length, weather, how often it rains and storms.
ControlsInput mode, hold to mine/build, mouse and touch look speed, invert look, gamepad, and the key bindings screen.
VideoGraphics preset (Low, Default, High), view distance, render distance, render scale, shadows, atmosphere, frame rate cap, field of view, UI scale, fullscreen, and Reduce flashing for lightning.
AudioMaster, music, ambience and effects volume, and mute when the window is in the background.
ExperimentsThings that are still being tried: third-person camera, Srclike (Counter-Strike style) movement, fire options, and the developer console.

Time of day and weather for the world you are in are under This world; they are saved with that world. Render distance applies when a world opens.

Questions and fixes

It is slow or hot.

Set the Graphics preset to Low. Or lower Render distance and Render scale, turn Atmosphere down, and cap the frame rate to 30 or 60. On a phone or tablet, the frame rate cap saves battery.

I cannot fly.

Turn on Allow fly in Settings, Gameplay, then press V.

There is no console.

Turn on Allow developer console under Experiments. The pause menu then has a Console button, and the backtick key opens it. See the Modding page for what you can type.

The camera drifts, or I am on the wrong input.

Raise Gamepad deadzone, unplug the controller, or set Input mode by hand.

macOS or Windows will not open the game.

The builds are unsigned. The steps for each system are on the Download page.

Where did my old Eden worlds go?

This is a separate app with its own folder. Copy the .eden files into the saves folder shown on the Download page and they appear in the list.

I found a bug.

Tell us on the Discord or open an issue on GitHub.
