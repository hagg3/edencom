Wonder Blocks (Emod example mod pack)

Base textures: "Voxel Pack" by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Taken from the 48x48 PNGs mirrored at
https://github.com/minetest-mods/voxelpack_by_kenney and scaled to 32x32 by port/tools/wonders-pack-art.py:
default_pine_tree, default_pine_tree_top, default_pine_wood, default_pine_needles, default_brick,
default_sandstone_brick, default_stone_brick, default_obsidian_brick, default_obsidian_block, default_ice,
default_clay, default_glass, default_stone, default_stone_block, default_mineral_gold.
Credit is not required by CC0, but given with thanks.

The icons drawn on top of them (house, bridge, swirl, question mark, sun, moon, cloud, cross, "i", sapling,
egg), the crate frame, the Lucky Block and First Aid backgrounds, pack.json, README.md and scripts/*.lua are
part of Emod.
